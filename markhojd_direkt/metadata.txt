qgisMinimumVersion=3.34

description=Get ground elevation from Lantmäteriet's Markhöjd Direkt service: click the map or generate an elevation grid or line profile points.
about=Click the map to get the ground elevation marked with a cross and the height value, draw an area to fetch elevation points in a regular grid, or fetch points along a line. Points are created as 3D points (PointZ, SWEREF 99 TM) and can be saved to GeoPackage, Shapefile or CSV. Covers Sweden only. Requires a system account (username/password) that has ordered the Markhöjd Direkt service from Lantmäteriet; there are no other external dependencies. The plugin is independent and not developed by or affiliated with Lantmäteriet; the name is used only to identify the data source. The user interface is in Swedish; an English explanation of all controls is in the README.
